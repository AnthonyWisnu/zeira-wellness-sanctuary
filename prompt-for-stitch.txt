### 1. VISUAL & AESTHETIC DIRECTION
- **Color Palette (Deep Forest & Mineral Linen)**:
  * Primary Canvas: Warm alabaster / bone paper (`#F9F8F5` or `oklch(0.98 0.008 85)`)
  * Deep Foundation / Typography: Rich obsidian slate (`#141716` or `oklch(0.18 0.012 160)`)
  * Botanical Accent: Muted deep olive-forest green (`#23382B` or `oklch(0.32 0.045 145)`)
  * Subtle Highlights: Warm sand gold / mineral ochre (`#C29B63`) for badges and verified status
  * Border Lines: Ultra-subtle hairline borders (`#E5E3DC`)
- **Typography & Scale**:
  * Headlines: Elegant, high-contrast modernist sans-serif (e.g., PP Neue Montreal, Cabinet Grotesk, or Satoshi style) with tight tracking (-0.02em).
  * Body: Clean, highly readable humanist sans with generous line-height (1.6).
  * Data & Meta: Tabular monospace font for timers, seat counts, and pricing numbers.
- **Atmosphere**: Architectural layout, generous breathing room (airy whitespace), soft diffused shadows (no harsh pure-black drop shadows), rounded micro-radii (8px to 16px).
